# Built-in ImageGen prompts

## iOS

Use case ui-mockup. NekoFin Chinese personal Jellyfin/Emby media client, second-round product design, three large legible phone screens side-by-side, premium restrained native app, warm neutral offwhite surfaces and charcoal text, restrained vermilion brand accent. All Chinese text accurate. NO promotional banners, NO cinematic hero images in settings, no invented profile. Functional settings hierarchy and generous touch targets. 3 matching subtle phone frames evenly spaced on a neutral background. Captions outside phone. Same information architecture across platforms. Concept includes proposed features, not screenshot of shipping app. LEFT settings root: title 设置, small understated NekoFin mark, grouped sections 观影体验 with rows 播放与画质 (subtitle 方向、默认画质与连续播放), 音频与字幕 (subtitle 语言偏好与字幕样式), 弹幕 (subtitle 显示效果与内容过滤); section 应用 with 外观 (trailing 跟随系统), 存储管理 (trailing 1.28 GB); section 支持 with 帮助与关于. Bottom nav exactly 服务器 / 搜索 / 设置, settings selected. No account here, accounts belong to server entries. CENTER 播放与画质 subpage: back navigation, group 播放 with 屏幕方向 value 跟随系统, 连续播放 switch on with helper 单集结束后播放下一集; group 网络画质 with 无线网络 value 原画优先, 蜂窝网络 value 最高 8 Mbps, helper 这是默认偏好，播放时仍可调整; group 兼容性 row 转码与兼容性 with chevron and subtitle 仅在需要时调整. No forced technical codec controls on first page. RIGHT 存储管理 subpage: back, restrained 1.28 GB total, two distinct sections 离线内容 row 下载管理 value 暂无下载 and caption 主动保存的影片，单独管理; 临时数据 rows 图片缓存 280 MB, 播放缓存 960 MB, 应用数据与日志 40 MB. Bottom button 清理临时缓存 plus helper 保留离线影片、账号与偏好. Do not put delete-downloads into this button. This is future design proposal, not claim download support exists. Platform: iOS native SwiftUI Form design. iPhone subtle titanium frames Dynamic Island. Large system SF-like title root, proper grouped inset Form lists with softly rounded section backgrounds, compact gray section headers, trailing iOS chevrons and current values, native iOS switch on center, SF Symbols, standard native navigation with back chevron. Native floating system-material tab bar, no handmade red dot selection. At least visually 44pt touch targets, 17pt body, no tiny type. iOS visual conventions over decorative brand gestures. Outside captions "iOS · 设置" "iOS · 播放与画质" "iOS · 存储管理".
